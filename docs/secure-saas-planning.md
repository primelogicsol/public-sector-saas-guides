# Secure SaaS Planning for Public-Sector Systems

Public-sector SaaS planning should begin with operational responsibility, not only software features.

## Core Planning Questions

1. What public or institutional function will the system support?
2. What records will the system create, receive, modify, approve, or publish?
3. Who is allowed to view, edit, approve, export, archive, or delete information?
4. What evidence must be preserved for audit, compliance, legal, or operational review?
5. What information should be public, restricted, confidential, or administrative?
6. What systems must the platform integrate with?
7. What reports, dashboards, notifications, and exports are required?
8. What continuity plan is required if the system is unavailable?

## Security-Aware Design Areas

- Identity and access control
- Role-based permissions
- Audit logging
- Data classification
- Secure hosting
- Backup and recovery planning
- Administrative review controls
- Incident response planning
- Vendor and contractor access governance

## Public-Sector Principle

A public-sector SaaS system should preserve accountability. Every critical action should be traceable to a user, time, role, record, and decision context.
